Welcome to ELSEWHERE

Your ratings, notes and history stay attached to your email on this device.

First time here? Use Sign up, not Log in. Owning the site is decided by the email stored in catalogue.js, not by who signs up first.

Passwords are salted and hashed with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (Web Crypto) in this browser. There are no demo logins and no seeded users — see PUBLISHING.md for the server-side version a public site needs.
